Our Expertise

Specialized Dental Services

Multispeciality dental care combining modern techniques, clinical expertise, and a patient-focused approach for healthy, confident smiles.

General Dentistry

Routine check-ups, preventive care, fillings, tooth extractions, and treatment of common dental problems to maintain your oral health.

Smile Designing

Personalized smile enhancement using cosmetic dental treatments designed to improve the appearance, balance, and natural look of your smile.

Orthodontic (Braces)

Braces and orthodontic treatment to correct crowded, crooked, or misaligned teeth and improve bite, function, and smile aesthetics.

Crowns & Bridges

Custom dental crowns and bridges to restore damaged or missing teeth, helping improve chewing function, appearance, and overall oral health.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Cosmetic treatments such as teeth whitening, veneers, and aesthetic bonding to enhance the appearance of your teeth and smile.

Oral-Maxillofacial Surgery

Specialized surgical care for complex dental and oral conditions, including difficult extractions, impacted teeth, cysts, and jaw-related problems.

Dental Implants

Dental implant solutions for replacing missing teeth, designed to restore chewing function, support, and a natural-looking smile.

Tooth Whitening

Professional teeth whitening designed to reduce surface stains and brighten your smile for a cleaner, more confident appearance.

Frequently Asked

Everything you need to know before your visit at ORO-Care Dental Clinic.

Is the dental treatment painful at ORO-Care Dental Clinic ?
Not at all. At ORO-Care Dental Clinic we use advanced anesthesia techniques and the latest equipment to ensure all procedures are as comfortable as possible. Most patients are surprised by how painless modern dentistry has become.
Which is the best dental clinic in Haltu ?
ORO-Care Dental Clinic, located at 118/1 North Purbachal Road, Kalitala (Near Tarke Bridge), Kolkata – 700078, is a trusted choice led by Dr. Shaileshwar Kumar (BDS, MIDA, FAGE). The clinic offers convenient timings—morning sessions on Mon, Wed, Fri & Sun (10:30 am – 2 pm) and evening sessions Mon–Sun except Saturday (6:30 pm – 10 pm)—making quality dental care accessible and hassle-free for the Haltu community.
How do I book an appointment at ORO-Care Dental Clinic ?

Do you offer payment plans or EMI at ORO-Care Dental Clinic ?
Yes, we understand dental treatments can be significant investments. We offer flexible payment plans for major procedures like implants and full-mouth rehabilitation. Please discuss options with our front desk.
How long does a dental implant procedure take?
At ORO-Care Dental Clinic implant placement itself takes 30-60 minutes per implant. However, the complete process including healing and crown placement typically spans 3-6 months. We provide a detailed timeline during your consultation.
Do ORO-CARE Doctors treat children ?
Absolutely! At ORO-Care Dental Clinic we have a dedicated pediatric dentistry approach. Dr. Kumar and our staff are trained to handle children with patience and care, making their dental visits stress-free and even fun.
