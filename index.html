Got Questions?

Frequently Asked

Everything you need to know before your visit.

Is the dental treatment painful?
Not at all. We use advanced anesthesia techniques and the latest equipment to ensure all procedures are as comfortable as possible. Most patients are surprised by how painless modern dentistry has become.
How do I book an appointment?
You can book instantly via WhatsApp by clicking the green button, call us directly at 9831211850, or fill out the contact form on this page. We typically confirm within 30 minutes.
What are your clinic timings?
Monday, Thursday, Saturday: 11:00 AM to 6:00 PM
Tuesday, Wednesday, Friday: 11:00 AM to 8:30 PM
Sunday: Closed
Do you offer payment plans or EMI?
Yes, we understand dental treatments can be significant investments. We offer flexible payment plans for major procedures like implants and full-mouth rehabilitation. Please discuss options with our front desk.
How long does a dental implant procedure take?
The implant placement itself takes 30-60 minutes per implant. However, the complete process including healing and crown placement typically spans 3-6 months. We provide a detailed timeline during your consultation.
Do you treat children?
Absolutely! We have a dedicated pediatric dentistry approach. Dr. Kumar and our staff are trained to handle children with patience and care, making their dental visits stress-free and even fun.
